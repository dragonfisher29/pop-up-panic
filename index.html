Pop-Up Panic

A free single-player arcade game in your browser. Close absurd pop-ups on a cursed 90s desktop before PanicOS 97 hits 100% RAM and crashes. No download, no sign-up.

How to play

  • Click or tap a pop-up's X to close it. Some pop-ups fight back: fake X buttons, windows that run from your cursor, countdown prizes, virus scans that multiply, and chains of "Are you sure?" boxes.
  • Every trick flashes a 300 ms warning first.
  • Force Quit (Space or right-click) closes the window under the cursor. Task Manager (Shift) closes every hostile window, once per run.
  • Daily mode: everyone gets the same seed. Your first run each day goes on the global leaderboard. Share your run as an emoji RAM strip.
